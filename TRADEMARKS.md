# AgentBro Trademark Guidelines

AgentBro is open source, but the AgentBro name, logo, app icon, website design,
and other brand assets are reserved by the project owner.

You may use the AgentBro name to:

- Refer to the original AgentBro project.
- Link to the official repository or website.
- State that your work is compatible with AgentBro.

You may not use the AgentBro name or brand assets to:

- Present a fork, modified build, product, or service as the official AgentBro
  project.
- Create confusion about whether your product or service is endorsed by the
  AgentBro project.
- Register confusingly similar names, logos, domains, or social accounts.

If you distribute a modified version, use a different product name and make it
clear that it is based on AgentBro.

For permission requests, contact the project owner through the official website:
https://www.agentbro.net
